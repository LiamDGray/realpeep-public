Frame-Level Hash Chain & Merkle Anchoring

Per-Frame Integrity Hash Chain

DEMO

Each video frame is hashed with the previous frame's hash, creating an append-only chain. Removal, insertion, reordering, or truncation of any frame breaks the chain. Finalizing a segment computes the Merkle root, which is anchored to the Bitcoin blockchain via OpenTimestamps.
