This page hashes frames from a public-domain NASA clip (or your webcam), builds a Merkle root, signs that root with a demo EdDSA-Poseidon key, and generates and verifies a Groth16 proof in a worker. It is the real proof path, not the animated simulation in the main demo.

Not hardware-attested. The TEE key is a demo key in JavaScript, the trusted setup is the repository’s development artifact, and the mesh / OpenTimestamps layers are not included. This demonstrates the client-side cycle cost and the frame-to-proof binding, not a production root of trust.
